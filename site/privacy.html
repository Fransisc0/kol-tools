KoL Tools Open TCRS viewer

PRIVACY

Private by design.

KoL Tools has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or application cookies.

Information stored on your device

The TCRS viewer stores display and filter preferences in your browser’s local storage. Those preferences remain on your device and are not sent to the application API.

Network requests

The viewer requests public game data from its read-only Render API. GitHub Pages and Render may process ordinary connection information such as an IP address as part of operating their infrastructure.

External links

Wiki links open the Kingdom of Loathing Wiki only when selected. That external site has its own privacy practices.
